Our Story

Africa's Creative
Marketing Partner

Jazika Concepts is a Kenyan-based Events & PR firm specialized in Marketing, Public Relations & Communications. Formerly Grand Concepts, we've been connecting brands with their people since 2014.

Our flagship brand, Afrika TV Kenya, boasts over 75,000 followers — giving us a unique ability to steer key social conversations on emerging issues among young Kenyans.

We treat every client — small or large — with the same passionate dedication. Every project gets a dedicated account director from start to finish, ensuring peace of mind and seamless delivery.
